How it works

Three steps. You do the detailing and the filming. DetailBot handles the ad side.

Pick a proven ad

Choose from ad templates and copy modeled on ads that already work for detailers, filled in with your services, your area and your offer.

Film and upload

Film the shots on your phone and upload them in the app. You can also upload a finished video you already have.

We run your ads

Connect your Facebook Page and ad account. DetailBot launches and manages the ads in Meta Ads Manager, aimed at people near you.

What you get

Everything lives in one app on your phone.

  • Ad templates and copy written for car detailing businesses
  • Your own videos turned into ads, with your business details
  • Ads launched and managed in your own Meta Ads Manager account
  • Leads from your ads collected in one place so you can follow up fast
  • Your daily budget is your call. Change it or pause your ads any time
  • Your ad spend stays in your own Meta ad account

What you need

  • An iPhone
  • A Facebook Page for your business
  • A Facebook ad account you're an admin of
